An Epistle From the Heart

My Dearest,

There is a kind of love that doesn't announce itself loudly. It doesn't arrive with fireworks or grand declarations. It sneaks in quietly through a smile that catches you off guard, through the way someone says your name, through a thousand small moments that, one by one, rearrange everything you thought you knew about yourself. That is what you did to me. That is exactly what you did to me.

I have tried I want you to know this I have genuinely tried to talk myself out of what I feel. I told myself it was too soon. I told myself I was imagining things. I told myself to be careful, to be smart, to not let my heart run ahead of my head. But the heart has never been very obedient, has it? And mine, it seems, has made up its mind completely.

So here I am. Bare and honest. Writing you words I've rehearsed a hundred times in the quiet of my own thoughts, finally brave enough to let you read them.

Why She is Unlike Anyone I've Ever Known

Beauty with soul

01
The Woman of My Dreams
I used to wonder if the woman I imagined existed outside of poetry. Then I met her. She is everything I used to quietly hope for patient, warm, joyful, and utterly real. She is not perfect in the storybook sense. She is perfect in the true sense: she is perfectly herself, and that is far more beautiful.
02
A Mind That Captivates Me
She is brilliant, and she doesn't even make a show of it. She speaks and I lean in. She thinks and I am left in quiet admiration. There is a depth to the way she sees the world a sharpness wrapped in gentleness that makes every conversation feel like a privilege. Beauty without brains fades. She will never fade.
03
She Works Like She Believes in Herself
I have watched her pour herself into everything she touches. She doesn't do anything halfway. She works with a quiet, unshakeable determination that is the most attractive thing in the world to me. She is building something her life, her future, her legacy and watching her do it takes my breath away every single time.
04
She Feeds You Like She Loves You
They say the way to someone's heart is through food, and perhaps that's how she found her way to mine. The way she cooks with intention, with care, with flavour tells you everything about who she is. She does not just make meals. She makes experiences. She turns an ordinary evening into something that feels like home.
05
She Is Good Genuinely Good
In a world that sometimes makes goodness feel naive, she is unapologetically kind. She cares without keeping score. She gives without asking what she'll get back. She is the kind of person who makes those around her want to be better not by demanding it, but by simply being the standard.
06
A Beauty That Runs Bone Deep
Yes she is physically stunning in a way that makes you pause and quietly thank the universe. But what makes her truly beautiful is that it goes all the way through. Her laugh. The way she listens. The softness she shows in private moments. The fire she carries in her eyes. She is beautiful in every single dimension a human being can be.
To the one who rearranged my world without trying,

I want to tell you something I've been carrying for a while now, something that has grown too big and too real to keep to myself any longer. I think about you in the mornings before the day starts. I think about you in the quiet between moments. You have become, without my permission and without any resistance from me, the person I most want to share things with.

True love, they say, is not the butterflies though God knows I have those when I see you. True love is the steady warmth that stays after the butterflies settle. It is choosing someone not just when it's easy, but especially when it isn't. It is looking at a person and thinking: with you, I can face anything. I think about you and I feel that. Not the panic of infatuation but the deep, calm, terrifying certainty of something real.

I admire you so much. I admire the way you carry yourself through hard days with grace. I admire your dedication to your work, to the people you love, to the life you are building. I admire the way you walk into a room and make it warmer just by being there. And I admire the quiet strength in you that you probably don't even see in yourself.

I am not here to tell you I am perfect. I am not. But I am here to tell you that whatever I am, I want to give it to you completely. My time. My attention. My loyalty. My laughter. My honesty. My patience on the hard days. My celebration on the good ones. All of it. Every last bit of it I want it to be yours.

You deserve someone who sees you fully not just the polished parts, but the real you and still chooses you with their whole chest. I see you. All of you. And I choose you. Without hesitation. Without conditions. Without an expiry date.

Until the poets run out of rhyme.
In other words, until the end of time.

Yours, always and completely

Today

Our First Chapter

Learning each other slowly

Late-night conversations that stretch into 3am. Finding our favourite restaurant. Learning how you take your coffee and which side of the bed you prefer. Building inside jokes. Being there for each other on the unremarkable Tuesdays and the broken Fridays. Learning you is something I want to spend a lifetime doing.

Not Too Far

Adventures and everyday magic

New places. New memories. Sunsets we'll still talk about years later. But also the everyday magic grocery runs, cooking together in a small kitchen, dancing in the living room for no reason, doing absolutely nothing and feeling like it's the best day of our lives. That is the life I want with you.

One Day Soon

A home that feels like us

A place where her cooking fills the air and laughter fills the rooms. Where we argue about small things and make up before sleep because we refuse to let the sun go down on our anger. A home where love is not just a feeling but a daily decision and where I make that decision, gladly, every single morning.

The Moment I've Dreamed Of

Down on one knee

I see it clearly. A ring I spent months choosing. Her eyes going wide. Me, barely holding myself together, saying words that feel completely inadequate for what I feel. And her saying yes. Making me the luckiest person who has ever lived. I am going to make that moment happen. I promise.

Forever

Growing old, still in love

Two people, grey-haired and still reaching for each other's hands. Still laughing at the same jokes. Still choosing each other not out of habit, but because love, when it is real, only deepens. That is the ending I want. And I want it with her. Only ever with her.

My promises to you

I

You will always know where you stand with me. My love will not be a mystery you have to solve or a game you have to win. It will be clear, consistent, and certain on the easy days and especially on the hard ones.

When the world is loud and unkind, I will be the quiet. When you are tired, I will hold you without needing you to explain. I will be the place you come back to always warm, always steady, always yours.
III

Every win, big or small. Every goal you chase, every wall you break through I will be the loudest voice in your corner. You will never have to shrink yourself around me. I want all of you. The bold, ambitious, brilliant all of you.

I will make you laugh, even on your worst days
Life is heavy sometimes. I know that. But I also know that laughter is one of the greatest gifts two people can give each other. I will be the reason you smile even when you didn't think you could. That is a promise I intend to keep for the rest of my life.
V
I will grow with you, not away from you
I do not want a love that stays still. I want us to grow individually and together. I want to become a better person because of you. I want to push you toward your greatest self. And I want us to look back, years from now, and say: we built something worth building.

I will choose you. Every single day.
Love is not just a feeling it is a daily decision. And I am making it now, and I will make it tomorrow, and I will make it every morning after that. I choose you. Not because I have to. Because there is no version of a good life, in my mind, that does not have you in it.
